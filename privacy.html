Juked Bot Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Juked Bot ("Juked Bot", "the Bot", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our") accesses, uses, processes, and stores information when providing its services through Discord.

2. Information Juked Bot May Access

Juked Bot may access information made available through Discord's API when necessary to provide its features. Depending on the feature being used, this may include:

  • Discord user and member identifiers
  • Discord server identifiers
  • Discord channel and message identifiers
  • Server roles and role information
  • Slash command and interaction information
  • Message content when required to process supported commands

3. Message Content

Juked Bot supports certain traditional text-based commands in addition to Discord slash commands. To recognize and process these supported commands, Juked Bot may access message content provided through Discord.

Message content is used to process Bot functionality and is not used by Juked Bot for advertising, user profiling, or unrelated analytics.

Juked Bot does not maintain a persistent database containing the general conversation history of Discord servers.

4. Server Member Information

Juked Bot may access Discord server member information when necessary to provide server and team management functionality, including functionality involving server members, roles, permissions, and interactions.

Member information is not sold or used for advertising or user profiling.

5. Counter-Strike and Competition Information

Juked Bot may retrieve information from third-party services relating to Counter-Strike 2 teams, players, matches, competitions, schedules, and game updates.

This information may be processed and stored when necessary to provide automated league schedules, match announcements, reminders, and Counter-Strike 2 update notifications.

6. Information Stored by Juked Bot

Juked Bot may retain operational information required to provide its features. This may include:

  • Tracked Counter-Strike 2 update identifiers
  • FACEIT or competition match identifiers
  • Team and opponent information
  • Match dates, times, and status information
  • Discord message identifiers for messages maintained by the Bot
  • Configuration information required to operate Bot features

Juked Bot does not maintain a persistent database of general Discord conversations.

7. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by Juked Bot is used to provide and maintain its functionality, including:

  • Processing supported Discord commands and interactions
  • Creating and managing scrim announcements
  • Providing role and team notifications
  • Tracking Counter-Strike league matches and schedules
  • Providing game-day and match reminders
  • Posting Counter-Strike 2 update notifications
  • Maintaining and securing the Application

8. Sharing of Information

Juked Bot does not sell Discord user data.

Discord user information is not shared with third parties for advertising or user profiling.

Information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, integrity, or operation of Juked Bot.

9. Data Retention

Discord information accessed for commands and interactions is processed as necessary to provide the requested functionality.

Operational configuration, match, schedule, and update tracking information may be retained for as long as necessary to operate Juked Bot.

10. Data Deletion and Privacy Requests

Users may request information regarding applicable stored data associated with their use of Juked Bot or request deletion of applicable stored information by contacting the Juked Bot administrators through the official Juked Discord community.

11. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by Juked Bot. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Children's Privacy

Juked Bot is intended to be used in accordance with Discord's applicable age requirements. Juked Bot does not knowingly collect personal information in violation of those requirements.

13. Third-Party Services

Juked Bot may interact with third-party services such as Discord, FACEIT, ESEA, Steam, and Counter-Strike 2 services. Use of those services may also be subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes to Juked Bot, its functionality, or applicable requirements. The "Last Updated" date above indicates the latest revision.

15. Contact

Questions, privacy inquiries, or data-related requests can be submitted through the official Juked Discord community.
